# Kaylee Schmid

## Continuity of Identity
I would say that I do not believe there is a form of continuum within a new physical body, but instead a continuum of the soul/consciousness. Thus, some or most traits of myself must stay the same for it to be my own soul or consciousness. Within this mindset, there is only one physical form I ever occupy – my current form. However, once my physical form fails, my soul will then ascend into a different reality.

## Repetitive Behavior That Changed Me
Once in my life when intention, repeated behavior changed me was when I started playing sports. Another thing that changed was when I locked in on studying and began to allocate certain times and locations to studying. This improved my cognition, I believe.

## Detachment vs. Discipline
Discipline, in my opinion, is what relates most similarly to my experience throughout life. My outcomes have seemingly changed various times on the flip of a coin. While this could be a display of detachment, I would more closely relate it to discipline, as such transitions often stemmed from a previous experience.

## Scarry on Consciousness
Scarry explores the primary idea of projection of sentience unto nonsentience objects. We do this for a variety of reasons, but mainly to aid ourselves in self-actualization.
