Journal

  1. Consciousness & Freedom

    I used to believe that consciousness was just the term for being awake. For example, when I wake up, I become conscious, as I have autonomy over my actions, will actively think, and store memories.

    I associate freedom with autonomy. There are many different sectors of freedom, with the most important one being bodily and mental freedom with the ability to choose what actions you do and what you think of. But on a larger scale, there is also freedom related to the set of rules one must follow in the society they are a part of (example: national laws).

  2. Continuity of Identity

    The concept of identity is very interesting. When I ask people to tell me about themselves (who they are) I will always care less when they mention their physical appearances, as they don’t get to choose many of those attributes. I care more about their inner self, what they like and dislike, their attitude on a daily basis. I classify one’s identity as the beliefs and interests they have, and since people can change throughout their lifetimes and even within a year, I lie closer to Montaigne’s belief than Plato/Gita. I once heard that the way someone acts is 20% DNA and 80% their experiences/environments so if there was a hypothetical memory wipe, they would act and be completely different.

    "A man cannot step into the same river twice, because it is not the same river, and he is not same man." -Heraclitus

  3. Repetitive Behavior That Changed Me

    Initially when I started working out, it was boring but I continued with the process so I could become stronger, and therefore a better hockey player. Yet, throughout working out and seeing progress and learning more of the science behind muscle building, I started to fall in love with the process of working on myself and it showed me that repetition makes even difficult tasks easier to do and learning the reasoning behind how concepts function may make the process actually fun.

  4. Detachment vs. Discipline

    The necessity of me going to the gym many times in order to develop the habit and gain a liking for working out shows how discipline applies. Detachment wouldn’t apply since my actions did in fact matter and my outcome wasn’t predetermined, I sculpted it based off my goals and my mindset.
